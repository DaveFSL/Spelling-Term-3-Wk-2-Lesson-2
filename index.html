Strategy Notes

Three tools for spelling long science words. Keep them handy for the splitter and the patch.

“ph” says /f/

When you hear /f/ in a science word, it’s often spelt ph, not “f”.

Remember: -sphere is always s-p-h-e-r-e.

Chunk it into syllables

Break a long word into beats you can say: hy · dro · sphere. Spell one chunk at a time.

Remember: tap the syllables — each beat is easier to spell.

Build from the root

Every chunk has meaning: hydro (water) + sphere (layer). Knowing the root tells you the chunk.

Remember: root knowledge + syllables = no more guessing.
